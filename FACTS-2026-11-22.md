# Facts release — 22 November 2026

Source: the project owner's decisions of 22 November 2026. This release changes what the site promises; the design and the page structure stay as they are until the new site replaces this one.

## What changed

- **Date.** "Planned launch: March 2027" becomes "Early access: December 2026" on the home page, in the answers, in the signup block and in the confirmation emails.
- **Artist visits.** Visits are not part of the first version. The menu entry, the home-page note, two answers, the proposal form and the city choice are gone. The page stays at its address with a short text and the announcement signup, and is closed to search engines.
- **Institutions page.** The visitor experience is described as the product works: three questions, then context with a source for every statement. The report lists the measures the product has: scans, devices, saves, questions and contact requests, by artwork, by day and by language. The example table with interaction time is removed. The first version is free of charge for venues.
- **Operator.** The privacy text and the footer name SM COUNTRY CLUB LTD. The privacy text is dated 22 November 2026.
- **Answers.** "What will be available at launch?" and "Will it be free?" follow the first version; TAGAI opens in the phone's browser, with nothing to install.

Not changed here: the headline, the "How it works" block, the two encounter stories, the images, the visual design. They change with the new site.

## Where the texts live

`content/facts-2026-11-22.json` is a second layer over the November copy, in seven languages, with the keys it retires. `scripts/prepare-prelaunch.mjs` applies it and writes `admin/server/content-seed.json`; baseline revision `2026-11-22-facts`. `content/previous-source.json` holds the hashes of the 11 November baseline, so untouched editor documents move to the new baseline.

Translations were written by an AI agent and read a second time by another; no native speaker has read them.

## Mail

Production already has `EMAIL_FROM` and `EMAIL_DELIVERY_ENABLED=1`. `ENQUIRY_RECIPIENT` and `EMAIL_REPLY_TO` were added on 22 November 2026. The site sends nothing until `RESEND_API_KEY` is set and a production deployment follows. Deploy this release before or together with the key: the earlier deployment would send confirmation emails with the March date.
